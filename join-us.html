Thinking About Joining?

If you are curious about Freemasonry or interested in becoming part of lodge life, this page explains what to expect and how to take the first step.

You do not need to arrive with specialist knowledge or certainty. Most enquiries begin simply with curiosity, a willingness to ask questions and an interest in learning more.

Who Enquires

A thoughtful first step, not a rushed decision

People approach Freemasonry for many reasons. Some are drawn by its traditions, some by the chance to meet others in a meaningful setting, and others by the opportunity for personal development and charitable work.

Joining should never feel rushed. A good first conversation helps you understand whether the lodge, its values and its rhythm of life feel right for you.

At St John In Bedwardine Lodge, an enquiry is simply the beginning of a conversation. It gives you the chance to ask questions openly and to understand what membership really involves.

Before You Enquire

Be Curious

You do not need to know everything in advance. Honest questions are welcome.

Be Genuine

The best enquiries come from a sincere interest in the values and community of the lodge.

Take Time

Joining is something to consider properly, not something to feel pressured into.

What To Expect

Questions people often have

If you are considering getting in touch, these are usually the questions people want answered before they make contact.

Do I need to know a Mason already?

No. Many people make an enquiry without knowing a member personally. The important thing is a genuine wish to learn more.

What kind of commitment is involved?

Lodge membership involves time, engagement and a willingness to take part. The details can be explained clearly when you enquire.

Is it right to ask questions first?

Yes. Asking questions is the right starting point. An enquiry is not a commitment to join; it is simply the start of a conversation.

The Process

How joining normally begins

Although every enquiry is individual, the first stages are usually straightforward. The aim is to help you understand the lodge properly before any decision is made.

You should feel informed, comfortable and able to decide in your own time.

Step 1

Send an enquiry

Use the contact page to introduce yourself and say why you are getting in touch.

Step 2

Have an initial conversation

You can ask questions, hear more about the lodge and understand what membership involves.

Step 3

Decide whether to continue

If it feels right, you can explore the next steps further. If not, there is no obligation.

Make Contact
