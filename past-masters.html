Honouring Those Who Have Served

This page recognises the brethren who have served St John In Bedwardine Lodge 7458 as Worshipful Master, preserving both recent memories and the longer record of service across the years.

The office of Worshipful Master carries both responsibility and honour. We are pleased to mark the contribution of those who have guided the lodge with dignity, commitment and care.
