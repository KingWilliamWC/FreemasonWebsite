About the Lodge

Rooted in Worcester and built on tradition, friendship and shared purpose.

St John in Bedwardine Lodge 7458 is a long-established lodge in Worcester, bringing together individuals from different backgrounds in a spirit of friendship, integrity and community.

Life at the Lodge

A welcoming lodge with tradition at its heart

Members meet regularly in a structured and respectful setting, where tradition, discussion and shared experience play an important role in lodge life.

Alongside meetings themselves, the lodge offers opportunities for friendship, conversation and mutual support, helping to create a strong sense of belonging within the wider Worcestershire Masonic community.

History

A continuing presence in Worcester since 1956

Since 1956, St John in Bedwardine Lodge has maintained a long-standing presence in Worcester. While rooted in tradition, the lodge continues to welcome those who value friendship, personal development and a positive contribution to the wider community.

The lodge reflects both the heritage of Freemasonry and its enduring place in modern life: bringing people together, encouraging personal growth and supporting charitable work at both local and national level.
